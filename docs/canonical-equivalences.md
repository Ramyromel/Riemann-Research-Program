# Canonical Reformulations of RH

This document records reformulations used as research interfaces. A reformulation is useful only when its equivalence is established with the stated hypotheses.

## 1. Critical-line formulation

RH:
For every non-trivial zero rho of zeta(s), Re(rho) = 1/2.

## 2. Completed-function formulation

xi(s) = (1/2)s(s-1) pi^(-s/2) Gamma(s/2) zeta(s).

Xi(t) = xi(1/2 + i t).

RH is equivalent to all zeros of Xi being real.

## 3. Positivity criteria

Li-type and Weil-type criteria convert RH into positivity statements on explicitly defined objects. The repository will treat the exact test-function class, normalization, and equivalence theorem as part of every proof dependency.

## 4. Spectral formulation

A Hilbert-Polya style construction would seek a self-adjoint operator whose spectrum encodes the ordinates of non-trivial zeros. Self-adjointness alone is insufficient: arithmetic identification of the spectrum is mandatory.

## Research rule

No criterion is used as a black box. Each imported theorem must be recorded with its exact statement, hypotheses, source, and role in the dependency graph.
