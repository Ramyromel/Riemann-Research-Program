# Active Frontier — 2026-09-20

## Purpose

This document records externally published work that materially changes the project's initial research strategy.

## Finding F1 — Weil positivity can be localized

A 2026 preprint by Marcus Chuk studies Weil positivity on compact support windows and gives certified two-sided bounds for the lowest Rayleigh quotient in compact windows. This shows that useful positivity can be certified on finite/compact sectors, while the global RH-equivalent positivity problem remains unresolved.

Project consequence: compact-window positivity must not be conflated with global positivity.

## Finding F2 — Finite truncated Weil forms have an exact zero-sum dictionary

A July 2026 preprint by Akiva Groskin establishes an exact finite Guinand-Weil dictionary for a class of truncated Weil quadratic forms. It also gives an explicit archimedean tail bound.

Project consequence: the finite-to-infinite problem should be decomposed into:
1. exact finite arithmetic/zero correspondence;
2. explicit tail control;
3. preservation of positivity under the relevant limit;
4. identification of the limiting form.

## Finding F3 — Numerical convergence is extraordinarily strong but not a proof

A May 2026 preprint by Akiva Groskin reports extremely high-precision approximations from truncated Weil forms, while explicitly reporting negative eigenvalues in finite raw spectra and making no RH proof claim.

Project consequence: numerical spectral convergence is useful for discovery but cannot establish continuum positivity.

## Finding F4 — Operator realization is not equivalent to RH by itself

A July 2026 numerical study of Suzuki's Weil-quadratic-form operator reports an operator realization and numerical spectral laws, while explicitly stating that it does not prove RH.

Project consequence: self-adjointness, numerical spectral agreement, or an attractive operator law is insufficient without an exact arithmetic identification theorem.

## Research decision

The highest-value near-term target is therefore not “find a beautiful operator.” It is:

> Construct a rigorous implication from finite/cutoff positivity plus explicit tail and limit control to the full Weil positivity criterion, while independently establishing the exact arithmetic correspondence required by the criterion.

This target is narrower, falsifiable, and compatible with the current frontier.

## Status

All findings above are external research inputs. They are not results of this repository.
