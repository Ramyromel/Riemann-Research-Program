# Open Gaps

These gaps must be closed before any RH proof claim.

## G1 — Positivity

Finite or restricted positivity does not automatically imply positivity of the full Weil quadratic form.

## G2 — Limit preservation

A sequence of finite objects may converge while losing the property needed for RH.

## G3 — Spectral identification

A self-adjoint operator is not sufficient by itself. Its spectrum must be proven to correspond exactly to the non-trivial zeta zeros, including multiplicities and normalization.

## G4 — Domain and operator issues

Candidate unbounded operators require precise domains, closure, self-adjointness, and boundary-condition control.

## G5 — Arithmetic correspondence

A proposed spectral model must explain the prime/Euler-product information rather than merely reproduce zero statistics.
