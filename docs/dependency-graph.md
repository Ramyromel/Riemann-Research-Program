# Proof Dependency Graph

The project treats a prospective proof as a directed dependency graph.

## Root target

RH: every non-trivial zero rho of zeta satisfies Re(rho) = 1/2.

## Required layers

A. Definitions and analytic continuation
B. Functional equation and completed xi-function
C. Explicit-formula / Weil machinery
D. Positivity or spectral theorem
E. Limit and domain control, where applicable
F. Arithmetic/spectral identification
G. Final implication to RH

## Acceptance rule

A node cannot be marked PROVED when any prerequisite is merely CONJECTURAL, NUMERICALLY_SUPPORTED, or BLOCKED.

## Failure rule

If a proposed implication is found false, record the counterexample and retain the failed route in the research log.
