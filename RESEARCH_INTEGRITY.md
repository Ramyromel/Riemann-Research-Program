# Research Integrity and Evidence Policy

This repository is intentionally public so that mathematical ideas, computations, failures, and verification work can be inspected by other researchers.

It does not claim a solution of the Riemann Hypothesis.

The official Clay Mathematics Institute description continues to list the Riemann Hypothesis as unsolved. This repository therefore treats the problem as open.

## Epistemic separation

Every substantive result must be distinguishable as one of:

- External fact/theorem — established outside this repository and cited.
- DERIVED — derived in this repository from stated premises.
- VERIFIED — independently checked against stated assumptions.
- NUMERICALLY_SUPPORTED — supported by computation but not analytically closed.
- CONJECTURAL — a proposed statement requiring proof or falsification.
- PROVED — a complete mathematical argument with all dependencies and limiting arguments closed.
- REFUTED — a counterexample or contradiction has been established.
- BLOCKED — an unresolved dependency prevents the intended inference.

## Non-inference rules

The following are not sufficient to claim RH:

- checking finitely many zeros;
- high-precision agreement with known zeros;
- positivity for one finite matrix or one parameter range;
- numerical spectral convergence;
- self-adjointness of an operator without the required arithmetic identification;
- an asymptotic estimate without the finite bounds needed for the argument;
- a finite-dimensional approximation without a rigorous globalization/limit theorem.

## External research

External papers and computational packages are recorded as research inputs. Their results are not silently converted into repository-proved theorems. Exact hypotheses, normalization, scope, and limitations must be recorded before an external result becomes a dependency of a claimed proof.

## Corrections

If a result is later found to be wrong, incomplete, or overstated, preserve the history and correct the status explicitly rather than rewriting the research record as if the error never occurred.
