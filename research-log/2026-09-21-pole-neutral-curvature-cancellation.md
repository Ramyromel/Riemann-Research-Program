# 2026-09-21 — Pole-Neutral Curvature Cancellation

## Finding

The external finite construction defines the even-sector moment
\[
M_0(v)=v_0+\sqrt2\sum_{k=1}^{N}v_k.
\]

Under the standard embedding \(u_0=v_0\), \(u_{\pm k}=v_k/\sqrt2\),
\[
\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}u=M_0(v).
\]

The exact prime-power derivative jump is
\[
-\frac{2\Lambda(q)}{\sqrt q\,\log q}\mathbf1\mathbf1^{\mathsf T}.
\]

Therefore its quadratic action is
\[
-\frac{2\Lambda(q)}{\sqrt q\,\log q}M_0(v)^2,
\]
which is identically zero on the pole-neutral family \(M_0(v)=0\).

The external pole formula is independently a positive square in the exact pole-neutral row, so the pole term also vanishes there.

## Status

This closes a structural subproblem: the rank-one singular arithmetic curvature is not a remaining obstruction on the pole-neutral family.

It does **not** close finite positivity. The remaining prime regular component and archimedean component still require an analytic sign theorem.

## Research consequence

The finite positivity target is now materially narrower:

Q_{\mathrm{restricted}}
=
Q_{\mathrm{prime,regular}}+Q_{\mathrm{arch},\infty}.
\]
